STUDIO CLEARANCE MEMO
Production: "Late Autumn" (2023)
Category: Brand / Product Placement
Entity: Coca-Cola

Decision: CLEARED WITH MODIFICATION

A Coca-Cola can appears in the background of the kitchen scene (Sc. 14) in an
unlicensed capacity. Legal counsel determined that incidental, non-promotional
background appearance of a real branded product does not require a licensing
agreement under fair-use / incidental-use doctrine, provided:

  1. The brand is not the subject of dialogue or a close-up shot.
  2. The product is not shown in a disparaging or defamatory context.
  3. No implied endorsement is created (no actor is shown consuming or
     praising the product on-screen).

All three conditions were met in "Late Autumn." No formal license was sought.
The legal team's rationale: courts have consistently held that background
product visibility in a narrative (non-commercial) work is not trademark
infringement absent confusion as to sponsorship.

Signed off by: Studio Legal, 2023-11-02.
Notes for future productions: this precedent applies only to background,
non-promotional use. Any scene giving a branded product narrative significance
(a plot point, a close-up, spoken dialogue naming the brand) requires a fresh
review and, likely, a real clearance request to the rights holder.
