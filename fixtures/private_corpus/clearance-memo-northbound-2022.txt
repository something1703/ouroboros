STUDIO CLEARANCE MEMO
Production: "Northbound" (2022)
Category: Music / Sync Rights
Entity: The Beatles — "Here Comes the Sun"

Decision: NOT CLEARED — REPLACED

The rough cut used "Here Comes the Sun" as a temp needle-drop over the final
montage (Sc. 41). Music supervision confirmed the sync + master rights for
Beatles catalog tracks are held jointly by two separate rights holders and
historically carry a minimum six-figure licensing floor for theatrical
release, with negotiation timelines of 3-6 months — incompatible with this
production's release date and budget.

Outcome: the temp track was replaced with a licensed, comparable-tempo
original composition before lock. No further action needed.

Signed off by: Music Clearance, 2022-06-14.
Notes for future productions: treat any Beatles-catalog needle-drop as a
near-automatic budget/schedule risk unless pre-cleared at the financing
stage. Do not lock a cut around a Beatles track without a signed license in
hand — this has been a recurring temp-track trap across multiple productions.
