STUDIO FACT-CHECK PRECEDENT
Production: "Static Orbit" (2024)
Category: Historical Event / Date
Claim: Dialogue states the Apollo 11 moon landing broadcast reached "over a
billion viewers worldwide."

Finding: DISPUTED — CORRECTED WORDING USED

Contemporary 1969 audience-measurement methodology could not have produced a
verified "over a billion" global figure — that number is a commonly repeated
but unsourced exaggeration. Nielsen-era U.S. estimates plus international
broadcast reach put a more defensible figure at approximately 600 million
viewers worldwide, the number most historians and broadcast archives cite.

Outcome: the line was revised from "over a billion viewers" to "hundreds of
millions of viewers" before final mix, matching defensible historical
figures rather than the popular but unverified round number.

Signed off by: Fact-Check, 2024-03-08.
Notes for future productions: "a billion viewers" is a recurring unverified
claim about the Apollo 11 broadcast across multiple scripts — treat it as a
known factual risk and verify against a real audience-measurement source
before it reaches a locked cut, not just at script stage.
