## Risks & mitigations
- **Marketplace subscription delays** → BYOK is the default; Marketplace is optional (E6).
- **Org policies blocking public Cloud Run** → use `--allow-unauthenticated` only for `webhook_receiver` (with signature verification) and the IAP-fronted dashboard; everything else is internal.
- **Agent Engine region availability** → keep everything in `us-central1`; Gemini via `global`.
